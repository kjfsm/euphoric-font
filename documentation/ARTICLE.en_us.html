Euphoric Pixel is a tiny pixel font drawn on a 5-dot-tall grid. Capitals are 5 dots tall, lowercase letters have a 3-dot x-height, and most glyphs are 3 dots wide, while m and w spread to 5 and i and l shrink to 1. Corners are filled rather than rounded, which gives it a square, blocky texture.

It covers A–Z, a–z, 0–9 and a small set of punctuation. It renders crisply on the web when the font size is a multiple of 8px.

To contribute, see github.com/kjfsm/euphoric-font.
